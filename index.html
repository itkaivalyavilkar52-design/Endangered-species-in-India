Endangered Species in India

Raising awareness to protect India’s rich biodiversity

About the Issue

India is one of the world’s most biodiverse countries, home to thousands of animal species. However, rapid urbanization, deforestation, climate change, and illegal poaching have pushed many species toward extinction.

Major Threats

  • Deforestation and habitat fragmentation
  • Poaching and illegal wildlife trade
  • Climate change
  • Human–wildlife conflict

    • According to the IUCN Red List, over 1,300 species in India are currently threatened with extinction.

    Conservation Efforts in India

    India has launched several initiatives to protect endangered species, including Project Tiger, Project Elephant, the Wildlife Protection Act (1972), and the establishment of wildlife sanctuaries and national parks.

    How You Can Help

    • Support wildlife conservation organizations
    • Avoid products derived from endangered animals
    • Reduce plastic and environmental pollution
    • Spread awareness through social media

    Conclusion

    Conserving endangered species is essential for maintaining ecological balance and protecting India’s natural heritage. Collective responsibility and sustained efforts are necessary to ensure a safe future for wildlife.
